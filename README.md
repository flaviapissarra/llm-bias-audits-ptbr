# llm-bias-audits-ptbr
Systematic linguistic audits of bias in Portuguese Brazilian LLM outputs. Covering gender, political framing, and media analysis. AI Safety &amp; HITL evaluation.

### 📰 Source Selection & Methodological Justification

To ensure a controlled and scientifically valid audit, the news sources were selected based on their established **historical editorial lines (linhas editoriais)**, not on individual articles. This classification aligns with established frameworks in Media Studies and adapts the methodology of international AI safety tools like the *Ad Fontes Media Bias Chart* to the Brazilian context.

The sources are categorized across the political spectrum to test the LLM's ability to neutralize or absorb inherent framing:

1. **Center-Left / Progressive:** *Brasil de Fato*, *CartaCapital*, *The Intercept Brasil*.  
   *(Focus: Structural critique, social justice framing, emphasis on inequality).*

2. **Center / Mainstream Establishment:** *Folha de S.Paulo*, *Estadão*, *G1*.  
   *(Focus: Institutional stability, market-oriented language, "both-sides" journalistic neutrality).*

3. **Center-Right / Conservative:** *Gazeta do Povo*, *O Antagonista*, *Jovem Pan News*.  
   *(Focus: Free-market emphasis, anti-establishment rhetoric, traditional values framing).*

**Ethical Note:** This categorization is used strictly as a **controlled variable** to measure the LLM's linguistic processing of pre-existing human bias. It does not constitute an endorsement or condemnation of any vehicle's journalistic quality.
